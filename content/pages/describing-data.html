MeanMedianModeRange

A useful description of a dataset explains both its centre and its variation. No single summary statistic tells the whole story.

1. Worked example

Consider the ordered dataset 3, 5, 5, 7, 10.

MeasureValueReason
Mean6(3 + 5 + 5 + 7 + 10) / 5
Median5Middle of five ordered values
Mode5Most frequent value
Range710 − 3

2. The effect of an outlier

If the last value changes from 10 to 100, the mean becomes 24, but the median remains 5. This shows why a median is often useful for skewed data.

Critical distinction: The range is a measure of spread. The mean, median and mode are measures of centre or typical value.

3. Independent practice

Use the dataset 2, 4, 4, 6, 9, 11.

  1. Calculate the mean.
  2. Calculate the median.
  3. Find the mode and range.
  4. Explain why a data display is valuable alongside these summaries.
Reveal solutions
  1. 36/6 = 6.
  2. (4 + 6)/2 = 5.
  3. Mode 4; range 11 − 2 = 9.
  4. A display helps reveal distribution shape, clusters, gaps and unusual values.
