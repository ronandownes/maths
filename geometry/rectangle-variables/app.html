Square and rectangle: definitions

Rectangle — Definition 30: A rectangle is a quadrilateral having right angles at all four vertices.

Square — Definition 32: A square is a rectangular rhombus.

A rhombus has four equal sides. So a square has four right angles and four equal sides: every square is also a rectangle.
