Split the Brackets

Understand the distributive law by splitting one bracket into two groups. Explore the four products, but do not confuse this visual explanation with the fastest routine written method.

Written mathematics

Strategic competence: splitting brackets makes the distributive law visible and can help explain factorisation by grouping. It is usually slower than direct expansion, a compact four-product layout, or a place-value method. Choose the quickest accurate approach you understand. For grouping factorisation, work directly towards the common binomial factor rather than writing unnecessary intermediate lines.

The scope here is deliberately binomial × binomial. Larger products belong in Place-value multiplication, not in this slow split-brackets method.
