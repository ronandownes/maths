Sine Ratio

Activities
Question settings

Find a side or an angle

Work from the marked angle. In the diagram, O is opposite, and H is the hypotenuse. Start with sin θ = O / H.

Understand the proportion: for a fixed angle, O and H scale together in similar triangles. When H is held fixed, O is proportional to sin θ. When O is held fixed, H is proportional to 1/sin θ. Inverse proportion is different from the inverse sine function.

Explore Pythagorean triples and their angles

A primitive triple satisfies O² + A² = H². Swap the two shorter sides to get the complementary angle. Angles are calculated rather than rounded to a preselected whole number.

The observed angle distribution depends on how the triples are selected. Twenty familiar triples do not establish a bell curve.
