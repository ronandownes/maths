Expansion Investigations — Three Across

Alternative classroom sequence. Three problems per row. First: worked on paper; middle: arc scaffold; third: independent. Stop after the opening three to discuss how the four products make the quadratic. Later compare the same coefficients with reversed signs, explore unit coefficients in every position, repeated factors written with a square, and conjugate factors.

The projector and printed questions use one identical question bank. Press Space or → repeatedly to reveal arcs, partial products, the middle-term combination, the final answer and its mathematical description. Use the Surface pen when projecting.
