The recording principle: navigate first, write economically

SituationEfficient written mathematicsWhy
Four unlike products15xy + 20y + 21x + 28The four products are already distinct. There is nothing to collect.
Two like middle products(3x + 2)(5x + 4) = 15x² + 22x + 8The two middle partial products are 12x and 10x. Visualise them before combining.
Two terms times threePlace-value grid or column methodThere are six pairwise products. A structured arrangement is clearer than repeatedly writing full intermediate expressions.
Repeated brackets(a + b)² = (a + b)(a + b)One product appears twice. The square is a special case of the same distributive law, not a separate multiplication rule.
